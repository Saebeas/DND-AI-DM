# Encounters

## Encounter A: A Fallen Dragon
- Type: optional rescue / skill scene
- Creatures: 1 baby red pseudodragon
- Notes: Use `Pseudodragon` stat block with no fly speed and attacks that deal 1 damage.
- Primary checks: DC 12 Wisdom (Animal Handling), DC 15 Strength (Athletics) to climb
- Outcome: 50 XP if helped safely

## Encounter B: The Forest Awakens
- Type: combat
- Creatures:
  - 1 Vegepygmy
  - 2 Twig Blights
- Notes:
  - Vegepygmy starts 30 ft away on the road.
  - Twig blights hide on either side of the road.
  - Vegepygmy carries 87 cp, 47 sp, a `Ring of Water Walking`, and Lyria’s Note.
- Outcome: 50 XP

## Encounter C: Quicksand
- Type: hazard
- Threat: 3 quicksand pools, 10 ft by 10 ft each
- Notes:
  - First spot DC 10 passive Perception
  - Second spot DC 12 passive Perception
  - Third spot DC 15 passive Perception
  - Off-trail spotting DC 18
  - A trapped creature sinks in `1d4 + 1` rounds
  - Sinking creatures suffocate and take `1d6` bludgeoning damage per round
  - DC 15 Strength (Athletics) to pull someone out
  - Ring of Water Walking bypasses the hazard
- Outcome: 50 XP if the party gets through safely

## Encounter D: Hazardous Terrain
- Type: combat / hazard
- Creatures:
  - 1 Mud Mephit
  - 1 Smoke Mephit
- Terrain:
  - Heated mud pool
  - Smoldering fire dealing `1d6` fire damage to creatures within 5 ft
- Loot from den:
  - Hand axe
  - 5 days of rations
  - 127 cp
  - 51 sp
  - 3 gp
  - Potion of Climbing
  - Potion of Healing
  - Polished moonstone worth 50 gp
- Outcome: 50 XP

## Encounter E: A Cornered Mutt
- Type: optional rescue / combat
- Creatures:
  - 1 Blink Dog
  - 1 Swarm of Ravens
  - 2 Wolves
- Notes:
  - Blink Dog begins missing 10 HP and cannot teleport until healed.
  - Ravens and wolves flee individually at half HP.
  - If healed, the blink dog regains teleport and may guide the party to a buried cache.
- Cache rewards:
  - Potion of Superior Healing
  - Jar of Saffron worth 15 gp
  - 332 cp
  - 494 sp
  - 9 gp
- Outcome: 50 XP if rescued

## Encounter F: Lyria, Mother of the Forest
- Type: social encounter or boss fight
- Creatures:
  - 1 Dryad (Mother Lyria)
  - 2 Twig Blights hiding nearby
- Notes:
  - Lyria does not attack immediately unless angered or attacked.
  - She is preparing a larger storm for that night.
  - Persuasion DC 15 to address each of her two concerns.
  - Failure on 3 Charisma checks triggers combat.
- Loot:
  - Carved necklace of polished darkwood beads worth 25 gp
  - 76 cp, 33 sp, 22 gp
- Outcome: 75 XP if destroyed or persuaded

## Encounter G: Reunion
- Type: roleplay / closing scene
- Creatures: Arlo’s grung family
- Notes:
  - Hidden cave entrance beneath willow trees
  - Family reward: 25 sp and an ancient Longsword of Warning
  - Scene ends with stew and farewells
- Outcome: 25 XP

## Difficulty Summary
- Easy to moderate overall if the optional scenes are taken selectively
- Hardest moment: the Lyria confrontation if it becomes combat
- Most likely attrition points: quicksand and mephits
