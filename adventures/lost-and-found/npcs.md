# NPCs

## Arlo
- Species: green grung
- Role: quest guide and lost child
- Personality: anxious, earnest, dependent on the party
- Motivation: get home safely and reunite with his family
- Notes: speaks broken Common; can navigate the forest trail

## Caulkin
- Species: human
- Role: Greenflower council member
- Personality: elderly, practical, wary of trouble
- Motivation: keep the village calm after the storm
- Notes: offers the escort job and 10 gp reward

## Mother Lyria
- Species: dryad
- Role: main antagonist / negotiable boss
- Personality: bitter, protective, thin patience, morally wounded
- Motivation: stop Greenflower's clear-cutting and defend the forest
- Notes: summoned the storm and may escalate into another one

## Arlo's Father
- Species: grung
- Role: family member
- Personality: chubby, squinty-eyed, jokey
- Motivation: welcome Arlo home and thank the party

## Arlo's Mother
- Species: grung
- Role: family member
- Personality: tall, big-eyed, nervous, wrings her hands
- Motivation: keep the family together and safe

## Arlo's Sister
- Species: grung
- Role: family member
- Personality: very young, playful, tiny vocabulary
- Motivation: react with wonder to the reunion

## Baby Red Pseudodragon
- Species: pseudodragon
- Role: optional rescue creature
- Personality: fearful, hungry, telepathically distressed
- Motivation: get back to its mother
- Notes: no fly speed; attacks reduced to 1 damage

## Blink Dog
- Species: blink dog
- Role: optional ally and rescue target
- Personality: intelligent, good-aligned, wary but helpful
- Motivation: survive the predators and help the party if rescued

## Vegepygmy Patrol Leader
- Species: vegepygmy
- Role: Lyria's forest minion
- Personality: hostile, fungal, obedient
- Motivation: stop the party from reaching the ritual site

## Environmental Creatures
- Twig Blights: forest ambushers under Lyria's control
- Wolves: starving predators drawn to weakness
- Swarm of Ravens: opportunistic scavengers
- Mud Mephit and Smoke Mephit: elemental hazards around the heated mud pool

## Roscoe "Cloudwatcher" Tealeaf
- Species: Halfling
- Role: local healer and acolyte at the Huckleberry Inn
- Location: Greenflower (Huckleberry Inn porch or near Council Hall)
- Personality: weary, gentle, superstitious, desperate but compassionate
- Motivation: ensure the party's survival; sees them as the town's last hope against the forest's wrath
- Notes: treats injured villagers after the storm; casts Aid at 2nd level on the party (+5 HP max/current for 8 hours); asks only that they "say a kind word to the forest" for him; offers Arlo dried fruit and sympathy, unlike other townsfolk

### Dialogue
- **Greeting:** "Caulkin says you're heading in? Bold or foolish, I can't tell which. But you look like you could use a bit of sturdiness against the wind."
- **Regarding the Storm:** "That wasn't just wind last night. Trees don't fall on houses by accident in this valley; they fall where they're aimed."
- **Casting Aid:** "Hold still. This won't stop a wolf's bite, but it might keep you standing long enough to bite back."

## Summary
The adventure's named NPC cast is small and readable: Arlo, Caulkin, Lyria, and Roscoe anchor the plot, while the rest of the encounters are built around forest wildlife and summoned threats.
