# Lost and Found

## Overview
- Level range: 1 to 2
- Expected table size: 4 to 6 players
- Estimated length: 1 to 2 four-hour sessions
- Theme: wilderness escort, environmental hazards, forest conflict, diplomacy vs escalation
- Setting: Greenflower, Lilygrove Wood, and nearby swamp/forest trails

## Adventure Premise
After a violent storm damages Greenflower, a young green grung named Arlo asks the party to escort him home through the woods. The journey exposes the consequences of the storm, the forest's dangers, and the resentment of Mother Lyria, a dryad who blames Greenflower's lumbering on the destruction of her home.

## Key Factions and Tensions
- Greenflower: a lumber village that sees the forest as a resource.
- Lilygrove Wood: a living forest scarred by clear-cutting.
- Mother Lyria: a dryad who has turned to violent storms and forest guardians to drive the town away.

## Adventure Flow
### 1. Opening in Greenflower
- The party wakes after a thunderstorm that damaged the village and a farmhouse roof.
- Caulkin, a village council member, asks the party to escort Arlo away from town for 10 gp.
- Arlo offers that his family can also reward the party, including an ancient sword.
- Roscoe "Cloudwatcher" Tealeaf, the town healer, finds the party on the Huckleberry Inn porch (or near the Council Hall via Caulkin). He casts Aid at 2nd level on the party (+5 HP max/current for 8 hours) as a desperate investment in the town's survival, asking only that they "say a kind word to the forest." He is the one person in Greenflower who treats Arlo with kindness, offering the grung a piece of dried fruit.

### 2. Journey Through the Wood
Arlo leads the party along a trail. The path can include optional side events and hazards:
- A baby red pseudodragon stranded in a tree
- Hostile forest minions patroling on Mother Lyria's orders
- Quicksand hazards in the marsh
- A heated mud pool with mephits
- A wounded blink dog fighting off predators

### 3. Confrontation with Lyria
- The party reaches a glade where Mother Lyria is performing a ritual.
- She can be fought, or persuaded to stand down through repeated Charisma (Persuasion) checks.
- Specific evidence from the journey can grant advantage.

### 4. Reunion
- Arlo reunites with his family in a hidden grung den.
- The family rewards the party and invites them to stay for stew.

## Major NPCs
- Arlo: young green grung forager separated from family by the storm.
- Caulkin: elderly councilman of Greenflower, cautious but practical.
- Mother Lyria: dryad antagonist, bitter but negotiable.
- Arlo's family: father, mother, and sister, all grungs.
- Roscoe "Cloudwatcher" Tealeaf: Halfling healer at the Huckleberry Inn; provides the Aid buff and represents the town's desperate hope.

## Structure Notes
- The adventure is designed so combat is avoidable in many places.
- The key social choice is whether the party resolves the conflict with Lyria peacefully.
- The ending can stop at Arlo's reunion or continue into a wider town-vs-forest political arc.

## Rewards and Advancement
- The adventure awards enough XP for the party to reach level 2 if the major beats are completed.
- Treasure includes coin, magic items, consumables, and the family's reward.

## Pacing Notes
- Front-load the town scene so the escort objective is clear.
- Use the optional wilderness scenes to vary tone and let the party choose what to engage with.
- Save Lyria's scene as the emotional climax.
- End with a warm, quiet reunion scene to reset the tone after the forest conflict.

## End State
The likely resolution is either a peaceful accord with the forest or a hard break that sets up future conflict between Greenflower and Lilygrove Wood.
