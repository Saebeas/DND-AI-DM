## Area 1: Mac the Apple Treant

- **Creature:** Apple Treant (Mac / Macintosh) — use Treant stat block
- **Disposition:** Grumpy, territorial about nature
- **Hostile Condition:** Only if party threatens trees/plants
- **Combat Style:** Aims to incapacitate, never kill
- **Special:** Can awaken 9 shrubs to pester the party at will
- **Information (DC 12 Investigation/Charisma):** Grammy chatted daily with dryads in the orchard
- **De-escalation:** DC 13 Charisma/Wisdom + plan

---

## Area 1: Goblin Patrols (Outside)

- **Creatures:** 1d4 goblins per encounter
- **Trigger:** Roll d6 every 10 minutes spent outside; encounter on 6 (goblins spot party first, try to flee) or 1 (party spots goblins first)
- **Avoidable:** Yes — goblins try to run away if they see the party first

---

## Area 2: Dryads (Apple Orchard)

- **Creatures:** 3 dryads
- **Disposition:** Mischievous, reclusive, hate goblins
- **Approach:** Won't appear without gift or DC 13 Persuasion check
- **Secret Recipe Location (DC 17 Persuasion/Investigation):** Half in office (Area 5), half in apartment (Area 9)
- **Loading Dock Intel:** If dryads like party, suggest loading dock (Area 7) for surprise approach
- **Hostile Behavior:** Pelt party with rotten apples if offended
- **Combat:** None — dryads retreat into trees

---

## Area 3: Violet Fungus

- **Creature:** Violet Fungus
- **Trigger:** Comes within 5 feet of waste pile
- **Speed:** Half speed when emerging
- **Avoidable:** Yes — stay away from the pile
- **De-escalation:** DC 13 Charisma/Wisdom + plan

---

## Area 4: Shop — No Combat

- **Locked Cashbox:** DC 12 Perception to find; DC 14 Sleight of Hand to pick; DC 16 Strength to smash
- **Loot:** 8 gp, 11 sp, 21 cp

---

## Area 5: Office — Trapped Drawer

- **Trap:** Poison needle — spotted DC 13 Perception, disarmed DC 16 Dexterity
- **Trigger Damage:** 1 piercing + 1d10 poison, DC 15 CON save or poisoned 1 hour
- **Hidden Safe:** DC 13 Perception to find; DC 15 Sleight of Hand to pick; DC 17 Strength to pry
- **Loot:** 75 gp, 50 sp, 25 cp; half the secret recipe; silver signet ring (10 gp)

---

## Area 6: Guard Room — No Combat

- **Locked Chest:** DC 15 Dexterity to pick; DC 17 Strength to smash
- **Loot:** 3 bottles of Shillelagh oil
- **Quarterstaffs:** Poor condition — d6 on failed attack, break on 5-6

---

## Area 7: Loading Dock — Stealth Encounter

- **Creatures:** 2 goblins on patrol (walking past, not in room)
- **Stealth DC:** DC 13 (averaged across party) — goblins pass by unaware
- **On Failure:** One goblin investigates, can lead to combat or social encounter
- **De-escalation:** DC 13 Charisma/Wisdom + plan

---

## Area 8: Bakery Floor — Goblin Pie-Makers

- **Creatures:** 3 goblins (drop from rafters)
- **Trigger:** Party interferes with magical baking equipment
- **Disposition:** Do NOT wish to fight; addicted to Grammy's pies, want the recipe
- **Negotiation:** DC 14 Charisma/Persuasion/Intimidation — agree if party shows them recipe
- **Deception:** Goblins easily deceived but will remember betrayal long-term
- **De-escalation:** DC 13 Charisma/Wisdom + plan
