# Grammy's Country Apple Pie — NPCs

## Tyndareus the Green (Quest Giver)

### Identity
- **Race:** Human (Elderly)
- **Role:** Wizard, Quest Giver
- **Location:** His tower (starting area)
- **Alignment:** Likely Good
- **Status:** Alive

### Appearance
An elderly man with long, wispy, white hair, milky eyes, and a large hooked nose. Lives in a tower filled with bookshelves, bubbling potions, mysterious trinkets, and ink-stained parchment.

### Personality
- **Traits:** Nostalgic, gentle, polite (offers tea to strangers)
- **Ideal:** The simple pleasures of life are worth pursuing
- **Bond:** Deep emotional connection to childhood memories of Grammy's pies
- **Flaw:** Might underestimate the danger to adventurers for a sentimental quest
- **Voice/Mannerisms:** Smacks his lips when talking about food, speaks slowly and deliberately, twinkling eyes

### Motivation
Wants to taste Grammy's Country Apple Pie one more time before he "departs for the Celestial Plane" (implies he is near death or planning to pass on). The pie represents his happiest childhood memories.

### Relationships
- **Imp Servant:** Loyal servant who can bake the pie impossibly fast once given the recipe
- **Grammy:** Remembers her fondly from childhood, never interacted directly
- **Party:** Hiring them; friendly, generous within his means

### Rewards He Gives
- Bag of Tricks (rust-colored)
- 25 gp
- A slice of the best apple pie ever baked

### Notes for DM
- Tyndareus has "little gold" — don't let players negotiate for much more
- He uses Mending cantrip to repair the torn recipe scrolls
- The imp servant is important for the finale scene
- His departure to the "Celestial Plane" is deliberately vague — could mean death, ascension, or planar travel

## Mac (Macintosh) — Apple Treant

### Identity
- **Race:** Treant (Apple Tree variant)
- **Role:** Guardian of the grounds, information source
- **Location:** Front entrance of bakery, near the path
- **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Status:** Alive

### Appearance
A massive apple tree near the path to the bakery doors. The bark looks like an ancient, wizened face.

### Personality
- **Traits:** Grumpy, old, impatient with "youngsters"
- **Ideal:** Nature must be protected
- **Bond:** Has lived on these grounds longer than anyone; connected to the land
- **Flaw:** Short-tempered, dismissive of those he considers foolish
- **Voice/Mannerisms:** Crotchety old man voice, uses phrases like "you kids," sighs heavily

### Motivation
Wants to be left alone. Will only help if treated with respect or if nature is threatened.

### Combat Behavior
- **Hostile Trigger:** If party threatens any trees or plants in the area
- **Combat Style:** Aims to incapacitate only, never to kill
- **Special Ability:** Can awaken 9 shrubs in the front of the building at will

### Information (DC 12 Investigation/Charisma)
- Doesn't know about any secret recipes
- Remembers that Grammy used to chat every day with the dryads in the old orchard
- Suggests the dryads might know something

### Relationships
- **Goblins:** Ignores them, they ignore him
- **Dryads:** Knows of them, suggests talking to them
- **Grammy:** Remembers her from when she was alive

## The Three Dryads (Apple Orchard)

### Identity
- **Race:** Dryad (x3)
- **Role:** Information sources, minor tricksters
- **Location:** Apple orchard behind the bakery (Area 2)
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Status:** Alive

### Appearance
Ethereal fey beings connected to the apple trees. Not described in detail — DM can embellish.

### Personality
- **Traits:** Mischievous, giggly, reclusive
- **Ideal:** The orchard is their home and they protect it
- **Bond:** Each other (they are a trio), and the memory of Grammy
- **Flaw:** Easily offended, childish when crossed
- **Voice/Mannerisms:** Whisper and giggle, throw apples, speak in riddles or half-truths

### Motivation
Want the goblins gone but are too reclusive to act. Entertained by adventurers but cautious.

### Interaction
- **Won't appear** without a gift offering or DC 13 Persuasion check
- **Secret Recipe Location (DC 17 Persuasion/Investigation):** Half in office, half in apartment
- **Loading Dock Intel:** If they like the party, suggest the loading dock for a surprise approach
- **If offended:** Pelt party with half-rotten apples until they leave or get bored

### Relationships
- **Grammy:** Used to chat with her daily — miss her
- **Goblins:** Disgusted by them
- **Mac:** Neighbors, likely aware of each other
- **Party:** Cautious but potentially helpful

## Goblins (Bakery Residents)

### General Info
- **Race:** Goblin (multiple groups throughout the bakery)
- **Role:** Residents / obstacles
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil (standard goblin)
- **Status:** Alive (unless killed by party)

### Goblin Groups

| Location | Count | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Outside patrols | 1d4 per encounter | Roaming, try to flee if spotted |
| Area 7 (Loading Dock) | 2 | On patrol, walking past |
| Area 8 (Bakery Floor) | 3 | Drop from rafters, pie-obsessed |
| Area 9 (Apartment) | 2 + 1 Chief | Defending their leader's room |

### Personality (General)
- **Traits:** Crude, curious, food-motivated
- **Ideal:** Grammy's pies are the greatest thing ever
- **Bond:** Each other (tribe), the pie recipe
- **Flaw:** Can't read, easily deceived, not smart
- **Voice/Mannerisms:** High-pitched, simple vocabulary, food-related exclamations

### Key Behavior Notes
- Have become addicted to Grammy's pies
- Have been trying (and failing) to replicate them
- Cannot read — see papers as kindling
- Will remember verbal information or demonstrations
- Easily deceived but hold long grudges about betrayal
- **Do NOT wish to instigate combat** — prefer negotiation

### Negotiation (Area 8 Goblins)
- **DC 14 Charisma/Persuasion/Intimidation** to get them to stand down
- Will agree if party shows them the recipe
- Cannot read but will remember things told or shown to them

## Goblin Chief (Area 9)

### Identity
- **Race:** Goblin (use Goblin Boss stat block or standard goblin with more HP)
- **Role:** Leader of the bakery goblins
- **Location:** Grammy's Apartment (Area 9)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Evil
- **Status:** Alive

### Personality
- **Traits:** Protective of his territory, proud of "his" bakery
- **Ideal:** The goblins deserve to keep the bakery
- **Bond:** His two goblin followers
- **Flaw:** Overconfident for a goblin
- **Voice/Mannerisms:** Deeper voice than other goblins, refers to self in third person, issues commands

### Combat
- Can be negotiated with or fought
- Has 2 regular goblin bodyguards

## Grammy (Deceased / Background Only)

### Identity
- **Race:** Human (deceased before adventure)
- **Role:** Legendary baker, original owner of the bakery
- **Location:** N/A (deceased)
- **Status:** Dead

### Legacy
- Famous for her Country Apple Pies
- Secret recipe split into two halves (Areas 5 and 9)
- Knew druidic magic (spellbook contains Druidcraft, Entangle, Purify Food and Drink, Speak with Plants)
- Had chilled storage rune variations inscribed in the stone rooms
- Was kind to the dryads and employed local young men as guards

### Notes for DM
- Grammy is never encountered alive — she's part of the backstory
- Her presence is felt through the still-functioning magical equipment, the recipe, and the memories of Mac and the dryads
- Can be brought up in NPC dialogue for flavor and nostalgia
