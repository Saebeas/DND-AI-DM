## Backstory
Elara spent decades studying at a prestigious academy of magic, specializing in the theory of planar energy flows. Her academic career ended abruptly when an experiment with a minor conjuration spell accidentally summoned a very confused goat into the dean's office. Seeking to restore her reputation through practical fieldwork, she set out to study magic in the wild.
