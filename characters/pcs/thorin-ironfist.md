## Backstory
Thorin was a blacksmith's apprentice in a small farming village. When a plague of giant rats swept through the granaries, he rallied the townsfolk and led the defense. The villagers still speak of his bravery. He left home to seek greater challenges and protect those who can't protect themselves.
