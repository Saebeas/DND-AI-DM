# Roscoe "Cloudwatcher" Tealeaf

## Identity
- **Race:** Halfling
- **Role/Occupation:** Local healer and acolyte at the Huckleberry Inn
- **Location:** Greenflower, Lilygrove Wood
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Status:** Alive

## Appearance
A stout, middle-aged halfling with curly, mud-streaked hair and a soot-stained yellow apron over simple robes. He carries a wooden holy symbol carved in the shape of a budding flower and smells perpetually of damp earth and herbal poultices.

## Personality
- **Traits:** Weary but compassionate; speaks with a gentle, tired voice; superstitious about the forest
- **Ideal:** Community survival above all; the forest must be respected, not conquered
- **Bond:** Has lived in Greenflower for decades; treated villagers injured by the storm all night
- **Flaw:** Exhausted and emotionally drained; "all out of bandages"
- **Voice/Mannerisms:** Soft-spoken, prone to sighing; wrings out muddy cloths or wrings his hands when anxious

## Motivation
Roscoe has watched the relationship with Lilygrove Wood sour as lumber harvesting grew more aggressive. He knows the town's survival hinges on this quest succeeding. He casts Aid not out of simple kindness but as a desperate investment—his last hope to keep Greenflower from burning or being crushed by the next storm.

## Relationships
- **Caulkin:** Respects the councilman; may send the party to Roscoe or lead them to him.
- **Arlo:** Unlike the hostile townsfolk, Roscoe offers Arlo dried fruit and a sympathetic pat on the head—one of the few in Greenflower who shows the grung kindness.
- **The Party:** Sees them as the town's best chance; asks only for a prayer to the forest in return.

## Stat Block (if combat-relevant)
Roscoe is not a combatant. He is a healer and support NPC.

## Notes for DM
- Roscoe should appear during the Opening in Greenflower scene, either on the Huckleberry Inn porch or near the Council Hall.
- He casts Aid at 2nd level when the party accepts the quest, granting each of the three PCs +5 HP maximum and current HP for 8 hours.
- He does not ask for gold. His only request: "Say a kind word to the forest for me, if it'll listen."
- He reinforces the theme that Greenflower is on the brink of crisis and not everyone in town is paranoid or hostile.
