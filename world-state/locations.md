# Locations — Grammy's Pies Campaign

## Known Locations

### Tyndareus's Tower
- **Description:** A wizard's tower filled with bookshelves, bubbling potions, mysterious trinkets, and ink-stained parchment. Accessed via a long winding staircase to the study at the top.
- **Connections:** Road to nearby town; road to Grammy's Bakery (2 days ride)
- **Notable NPCs:** Tyndareus the Green, Imp Servant
- **Status:** Intact, occupied
- **Notes:** Quest starting point

### Nearby Town (Unnamed)
- **Description:** A small town near the bakery. Stocks general goods (PHB equipment, no magic items).
- **Connections:** Tyndareus's tower; Grammy's Bakery
- **Notable NPCs:** None established
- **Status:** Normal
- **Notes:** Resupply stop before the bakery

### Grammy's Bakery
- **Description:** A large stone building surrounded by an overgrown lawn and old apple orchard. Front doors are loosely barred from inside. Goblins use the back entrance.
- **Connections:** Nearby town; road to Tyndareus's tower
- **Notable NPCs:** Mac (treant), 3 dryads, multiple goblins, violet fungus
- **Status:** Abandoned/overrun by goblins
- **Notes:** Contains 9 areas — see adventures/grammys-country-apple-pie/adventure.md

### Apple Orchard (Behind Bakery)
- **Description:** Older trees in neat rows, overgrown with saplings. Thick apple scent, whispering sounds.
- **Connections:** Grammy's Bakery grounds
- **Notable NPCs:** 3 dryads
- **Status:** Overgrown
- **Notes:** Key information source — dryads know recipe is split between office and apartment
