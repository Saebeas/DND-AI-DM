## Backstory
Lyra made a comfortable living running fake potion scams across the countryside. Her favorite trick: selling colored water as "elixirs of beauty." When one of her marks turned out to be a merchant with connections to the thieves' guild, she decided it was time to leave town quickly. She's always looking for the next angle.
