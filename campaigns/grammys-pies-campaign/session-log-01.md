## Session Narrative

### The Wizard's Tower
The party arrived at Tyndareus's tower and was greeted by his imp servant. They ascended to the wizard's study, where the elderly Tyndareus the Green offered them tea and explained his quest: retrieve the secret apple pie recipe from the abandoned Grammy's Bakery, now overrun by goblins.

Thorin spat out the tea, expecting ale. Tyndareus offered him a 40-year-old dwarven single malt instead.

### Negotiation
Thorin pushed for a better deal. Tyndareus countered at 60 gold + whisky. Thorin held firm at 75 gold — **Persuasion check DC 13: Thorin rolled 18. Success.**

Final deal struck: 75 gold, Bag of Tricks, 3 Potions of Healing, bottle of 40-year-old dwarven whisky, and a map to the bakery.

### Departure
Party accepted the quest, shook hands with Tyndareus, and departed. Tyndareus warned them about the goblins and mentioned that the apple tree out front is named Mac and is "particular."
