## Pre-Adventure Status
The three have been traveling together for nearly a month, forming an unlikely but effective team.
Sudafed and Stabitha: They met in a bustling city several weeks ago. Sudafed, having recently abandoned his "gilded cage" at the Archives of Aethelgard, was attempting to "democratize" knowledge by reading aloud from a restricted book to a group of street urchins. Stabitha, always looking for a mark, tried to pickpocket the "distracted" elf, only to be caught by his keen insight. Instead of turning her in, Sudafed—impressed by her "cheeky" spirit—bought her a meal and invited her to see the "open road" she had always dreamed of
.
Toby Two-Lunch Joins the Fray: The duo encountered Toby a week later at a military outpost. Toby earned his nickname as a young recruit; a massive bully named Grogmar would steal Toby's rations every single day. Tired of being hungry but too disciplined to start a brawl, Toby simply started packing two lunches—one for the bully to take, and one for himself to eat in peace. This habit followed him even as he transitioned from a soldier to a druid, finding that the natural world was far more generous than the barracks. Sudafed was impressed by Toby’s pragmatic "sharing," and Toby was drawn to Sudafed’s mission to protect the common folk.
The Intro: Why They Are in Greenflower
The party arrived in Greenflower yesterday afternoon, having been hired to defend a merchant caravan traveling through the "untamed frontier" of the Lilygrove Wood. The caravan was headed elsewhere, but the thunderous storm that swept through the village forced everyone to take cover.
The party spent a miserable, sleepless night in the Huckleberry Inn as the wind shook the building to its foundation
