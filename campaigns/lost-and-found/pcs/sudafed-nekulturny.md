## Personality, Background & Backstory
- **Personality Traits:** Explains complex magical theories using metaphors about farming or tavern brawls so everyone can understand. Knowledge is a right, not a privilege.
- **Ideals:** Knowledge is a right, not a privilege. If a law prevents a person from learning or surviving, that law is unjust.
- **Bonds:** I carry a specific book from my old library. It's filled with my own notes on how to actually help people, and I will protect it.
- **Flaws:** I have zero patience for refined etiquette. If someone is being a jerk, I will tell them, regardless of their status.
- **Allies & Organizations:** Loosely associated with a network of traveling teachers or rebels who smuggle books out of private collections to give to commoners.

### Backstory
Sudafed spent over a century as a Senior Archivist in the Archives of Aethelgard, a floating elven library dedicated to the preservation of "High Culture". With his proficiency in Arcana and History, he was a master of the stacks, spending his days practicing Calligraphy and transcribing ancient Draconic texts. He was the picture of elven refinement: 6'5", elegant, and silent.

The shift occurred when a neighboring human settlement was devastated by a magical blight. While the common folk starved, the High Sages of Aethelgard debated whether the blight was a "sub-variant of third-cycle necromancy" rather than sending aid.

Sudafed's patience snapped. During a gala, he stood on a mahogany reading table—boots covered in street mud—and shouted that their "culture" was nothing but a gilded cage for cowards. The High Sage sneered, calling him "Nekulturny" (uncultured) for his outburst. Sudafed accepted the title as a badge of honor, packed his Chain Mail, grabbed a Battleaxe, and walked out the front gates.

He didn't leave his books behind entirely; he still carries a heavy Book from the restricted section. However, instead of reading it in silence, he now uses his Magic Initiate abilities to protect those who can't protect themselves. He believes that if knowledge doesn't serve the living, it is useless.

Now 38 years into his "second life," Sudafed is a strange sight. He still has the striking Grey skin and Blue hair of his lineage, but his Robe is tattered, and his Shield is dended from blocking blows for villagers. He will use his Calligrapher's Supplies to write a perfectly phrased, beautiful insult to a corrupt lord. In combat, he uses his Longsword with the Sap mastery not just to hurt, but to humiliate enemies by "correcting" their poor form. He uses Prestidigitation to keep his companions' water clean rather than for flashy displays.

Sudafed is looking for a way to "democratize" the hoard of knowledge locked away in places like Aethelgard. He follows his own heart (Chaotic Good), believing that a well-placed Battleaxe to topple a tyrant does more good for the world than a thousand years of quiet study.
