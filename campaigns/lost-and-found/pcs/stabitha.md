## Backstory

An urchin child orphaned at a young age survives on the streets with a bit of luck and a lot of sneaking and thieving. She pickpockets a guildmaster and is caught, but the guild master is impressed and takes her in. She is raised as a rogue in the city but dreams of adventure and the open road.
