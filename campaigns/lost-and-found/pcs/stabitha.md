## Class Features
- **Expertise:** Double proficiency bonus on two chosen skill proficiencies (Sleight of Hand, Stealth).
- **Sneak Attack:** Once per turn, deal extra 1d6 damage on a hit with a Finesse/Ranged weapon if you have Advantage or an ally is within 5 ft. of the target.
- **Thieves' Cant:** You know Thieves' Cant and one other language (Gnomish).
- **Weapon Mastery:** Dagger (Nick), Shortbow (Vex).

## Species Traits
- **Brave:** Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
- **Halfling Nimbleness:** Can move through the space of any creature one size larger.
- **Luck:** When you roll a 1 on a d20, you can reroll and must use the new roll.
- **Naturally Stealthy:** Can Hide when obscured only by a creature at least one size larger.

## Feats
- **Alert:** +2 to Initiative rolls. Can swap Initiative with a willing ally after rolling.

## Appearance
Small halfling woman, age 20, 3 ft. tall, 40 lbs. Fair skin, moss-green eyes, chestnut tangled hair. Always barefoot.

## Backstory & Personality
**Backstory:** An urchin child orphaned at a young age, surviving on the streets with luck and sneaking. She pickpocketed a guildmaster and was caught, but the guildmaster was impressed and took her in. Raised as a rogue in the city, she dreams of adventure and the open road.

**Personality Traits:** Curious, mischievous, cheerful, optimistic, quick-witted, brave, cheeky, food-focused. Prone to skipping and frolicking, often laughs to herself. Barefoot at all times.

**Ideals:** Hoping to find Tymora's coin — a powerful luck charm that always lands right side up.

**Bonds:** Loyal to friends, not highfalutin ideals.

**Flaws:** When she sees something valuable, she can't think of anything except how to steal it.

**Faith:** Tymora
